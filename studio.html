The practice

Two architects. One shared obsession.

TWO Architects is an Ahmedabad-based architecture firm founded in 2019 — a young, enthusiastic and creative team providing architectural, interior and landscape design services across the globe.

Founders

Art and architecture,
detail and delivery.

Ar. Nil Patel

Co-Founder & Creative Partner

Nil earned his Bachelor’s degree in Architecture from Parul University, Vadodara in 2018. He completed his internship at HPA (Hiren Patel Architects) and gained his experience in design and technical understanding at VMS, Ahmedabad.

He co-leads TWO’s team to match the goals of each project, handling every one with a harmonious coupling of art and architecture and a deep passion for aesthetic improvisation and composition.

Ar. Shweta Dave

Co-Founder & CFO

Shweta earned her Bachelor’s degree in Architecture from Parul University, Vadodara in 2018. She completed her internship at Banaji & Associates, Mumbai and gained her design experience at Design Square Architects, Ahmedabad.

Blending conceptual design development with a mastery of detail, her work satisfies complex project requirements and creates spaces that combine comfort and utility. She was recognised as Student of the Year, 2015, during her summer internship at Politechnika Wrocławska, Poland.
